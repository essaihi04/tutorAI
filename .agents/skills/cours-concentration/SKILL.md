# Cours en mode concentration

Every course is now made like the waves example (`backend/data/courses/phys_ch1_waves_exemple.json`, rules validated by the user on 8 November 2026). The student does not read while the teacher speaks: he looks. Nothing goes to the database or the live site before the user has validated the course locally.

## The screen (already built: `frontend/src/components/course/CourseFocusStage.tsx`)

- **Top:** the slide title and its subtitle (`screen_content.lead`), nothing else.
- **Centre:** the image, the chalk simulation or the slide's table; formulas (`concentration.centre`) appear when the voice says them.
- **Right column (200 px):** the 3D tutor (`AIAvatar discret`), no toolbar, no label; he stays in his column and points with arm and pointer — he never stands on the writing.
- **Questions board:** when the voice ends, a second chalk board slides down from the top over the scene (« ✏️ À toi · N questions »): ONE question at a time, in a carousel (« ← Précédent », dots, « Suivant → »), never scrolling: the student answers, hears the reaction, then moves to the next (it also moves by itself 4,5 s after a question is finished); the dots turn green for answered questions. Each answer gets its prepared voiced reaction and is recorded for the acquis.
- **After the questions:** all right → the board closes and the course moves on by itself; one wrong → the reprise cycle on the same slide (re-explain another way with the tutor piloting the figure, then a twin question; three cycles at most, then the answer, the notebook line and the competence marked not acquired) — professeur rule 22. A hand move to another slide during a cycle marks the competence not acquired too.
- **Bottom bar:** play/pause (also pauses the simulation), replay, « 🙋 Question » (the student asks in writing, by voice or with an image; the tutor answers in context — professeur rule 23), « 📓 À écrire dans le cahier » (opens on demand only; starts with the title and subtitle, then the trace), « 📖 Explication écrite » (full content and the spoken text), « ✏️ À toi » (opens by itself when the voice ends).
- **The student's question is answered at the board, not in text** (user rule, 9 November 2026 — professeur rule 23): the question is classed against the course plan — this slide (explained on a board under the figure, lines written with the voice), a slide still to come (pre-recorded « on la traite bientôt », title of that slide, back to the current slide), a basic notion of previous years (the board is erased with a brush during a pre-recorded sentence, then explained on the clean board), another course or subject (pre-recorded « pas dans ce cours », no answer). The text is shown only on demand. After each explanation the tutor asks « tu as bien compris ? » and checks with a small exercise written on the board (association, vrai/faux or QCM, chosen by the case), recorded in the acquis. Nothing to prepare per course, but the plan only works if every slide has a clear `title` and `screen_content.lead` (they are what the classifier reads) and the deck is a local manifest (`data/courses/*.json`, read by `suivi_acquis.charger_deck`).
- **The student's own result on a simulation is shown only on demand** (user rule, 10 November 2026). When the student changes a setting and gets a new result, nothing opens over the board: a button blinks at the bottom (« 🔎 Ton résultat »), the course goes on. Only when he presses it: the course pauses, the panel opens, and **everything written in it is said by the tutor** (the text appears at the rhythm of the voice, nothing written is left unsaid). When the voice ends, a small evaluation like the course questions (QCM or vrai/faux, every option with its own feedback, spoken); once answered and the feedback said, the panel **closes by itself and the course resumes by itself**.
- **A question that needs a calculation or a reasoning gets its handwritten demonstration** (user rule, 10 November 2026). Right or wrong, once the student has answered (and heard the reaction), the board writes the demonstration line by line in handwriting while the tutor says it — each line appears on its word, like the formulas in the centre — then the course moves to the next question or slide by itself. Data: `question.demonstration = {parole, lignes: [{texte, mot}], audio}` (same on an exercise item); `parole` follows the voice rules (Bodi voice), every `mot` is an exact extract of it, `lignes` are written as the marking scheme reads (relation, substitution, result with unit).
- **A free (open) answer can be written, spoken or photographed** (user rule, 10 November 2026). Spoken: the micro (MoulSot), the transcription is shown. Photographed: the photo of the student's sheet is read by OCR (`ocr_service`: Mistral OCR, then DeepSeek normalises it with the slide's context — symbols, units, notation of the chapter). In every case the text is shown in the field and the student **corrects it by hand before sending**. A **« ⌨ Symboles » button** opens a virtual scientific keyboard (physics, chemistry, maths of the whole 2 Bac PC: Greek letters, indices and exponents, fractions, roots, arrows of reactions, vectors, units, ∞, ∈, ∫, lim…) that inserts at the cursor; it is in every field where the student writes (answer, free question).
- Local check: `http://localhost:5173/dev/course-player?scene=<scene>&diapo=<slide id>` (`&mode=tableau` = old board).

## Build a course, in this order

0. **Explore first** (skill `exploration-de-cours`, user rule, 9 November 2026): ten YouTube lessons of ten Moroccan teachers audited and compared (transcripts, board images, students' comments), the cadre de référence, the exam mistakes, every complex notion cut into small steps, the simulations and lab manips to plan. The plan below is written from `backend/data/curriculum/exploration/<clé>.json`, never from memory; order of the chapters: `programme_semestre1.json`.
1. **Competences first** (skill `suivi-des-acquis`): cut the chapter into 6–10 competences (`deck.competences = [{id, label, slide_ids}]`, labels in the bac's verbs), give every slide its `competence_ids`. The tracking of the student's acquis is done at this grain; a course without competences is tracked per activity only.
2. **Content per slide:** title, lead, bullets, essential text, student trace, table; the question (`question.prompt`, `options`, **`correct`** = index, options shuffled so the right one is not always first) and the exercises (QCM with `correct`, vrai/faux with `correct` per statement, `explanation`). **Every question or exercise that asks to compute or to show something carries its `demonstration`** (`{parole, lignes: [{texte, mot}]}`, written before the voice step so it is voiced with the rest).
3. **Visual per slide.** Every phenomenon that moves is a **chalk simulation with a transparent background**, never a static picture (skill `simulations-au-tableau`; example: the drop and the ball, `ondes-tableau/ballon.html`). Each simulation exposes named `moments` computed from its physics, accepts `play_until {moment}`, `set_time`, `set_parameters`, `highlight {target}`, says `ce_qui_se_voit` and `derniere_main` (tutor or student). A static figure is only for what does not move (skill `scientific-course-visuals`).
4. **Spoken text** (`speech_text.mixed`) — skill `voix-du-cours`: when the visual has several views (dessus / côté / graph y(x) / y(t)) or a direction word (transversale, longitudinale), the text FIRST says what each view shows and where the eye is, with a `pointage` on each view (professeur rule 21); simple darija, French for programme terms, equations, numbers and units in words, no digits/symbols/tashkil, every all-French sentence over ten words cut by commas; checked against the voice corpus.
5. **Concentration data per slide** (`concentration`, type `SlideConcentration` in `frontend/src/components/course/types.ts`); every `mot` is an exact extract of the spoken text (its position gives the instant):
   - `demarrer`: the command the tutor presses when the voice starts (`start`, `fire_pulse`);
   - `pointage`: `{mot, repere}` (landmark drawn in the simulation), `{mot, cible: 'visuel' | 'ligne:N' | 'centre:N'}`, `{mot, commande: 'play_until', parametres: {moment}}` — **whatever the voice describes, the simulation shows at that moment** (professeur rule 18): one freeze per phase said (monte → descend → revient);
   - `centre`: `{texte, mot}` formulas written when said;
   - `cahier`: the trace to copy;
   - `variante`: what the tutor varies himself 5 s after the voice if the student did not touch the scene (`set_parameters` + `play_until` + `highlight`) — professeur rule 19.
6. **Voice tracks** — skill `voix-du-cours`, **always the Bodi voice** (`academy-v5-bodi`, test kernel, script on the VPS: `voix-du-cours` section 3; user rule, 10 November 2026 — the Amine route `gen_kaggle_cours_exemple.py` is no longer used), `detecter_murmures.py` must print only `ok`, then `poser_pistes_exemple.py <out> <version>`; bump the version, delete the old files.
7. **Prepared reactions to every possible answer** (professeur rule 20) — `C:/Users/HP/Desktop/voxtts/corpus_v4/preparer_reponses.py`:
   - `items <cours.json> <items.json>` → for each option of each question: `juste`, or `1` (what is right + one hint, no answer) and `2` (answer + justification);
   - on the VPS, `gen_reponses_vps.py <items> <sortie>` (DeepSeek, same prompt as the live tutor, verdict imposed by the key);
   - `poser <cours.json> <sortie> <pistes.json>` → `concentration.reponses[cle][option][juste|1|2] = {parole, ecrit}`;
   - `python gen_kaggle_cours_exemple.py reponses=<pistes.json>` (kernel `academy-cours-reponses`), murmur check, then `audio <cours.json> <out> <version>` → each reaction gets its `audio` track. The player then answers **instantly, with no server**.
   - Open questions (no options) and the student's own manipulations of a simulation cannot be enumerated: they go live to DeepSeek (`/course-player/answer-reaction`, `/course-player/sim-reaction`) and the live voice.
8. **Second control — the cadre de référence** (user rule, 8 November 2026): `python C:/Users/HP/Desktop/voxtts/corpus_v4/controler_cadre.py <cours.json> <chapitre>` against `backend/data/curriculum/cadre_reference_2bac_pc.json` (one sheet per chapter: capacities, formulas as they must be written and said, keywords, out-of-scope words). It must print « cadre : OK » : every competence carries `cadre_ids`, every capacity is worked, every written formula is a cadre formula (or a numeric application), no out-of-scope word in the writing nor in the voice (unless announced as « plus tard »), and the voice reads each formula the way the cadre says. A chapter without a sheet gets one first, from the official cadre de référence and the Moroccan textbooks. **Formulas are checked against the teachers' boards**: for any doubt or variant (τ = SM/v vs Δt, how indices are written, d = v·τ/2…), extract frames of YouTube lessons of Moroccan teachers (yt-dlp + ffmpeg, then read the frames with the Read tool — no OCR engine is installed) and keep the writing the teachers use; record the decision in the sheet (`variantes_admises`). Indices are never written with « _ » for the student: `y_M(t)` in the data is rendered with M as a subscript (chalk `Craie.segments`, screen `<Formule>`).
9. **Close the course with the bilan, two slides** (user rule, 8 November 2026, model: `wx_b3_bilan` and `wx_b4_carte`):
   - **« Ce qu'il faut retenir »** = a chalk fiche (`ondes-tableau/bilan.html`) on **ONE page, two pages at most**: one block per part of the course (6–8), each with the definition to learn by heart (French, word for word, as the cadre writes it), the formula in a frame (indices rendered), and the trap in orange (« ⚠ »). The voice goes block by block, each cue is `{mot, commande: 'play_until', parametres: {moment: <bloc>}}`: the block lights up and writes itself, the blocks already said are written at once. **Page 2 is the student's page**: what they still have to rework. The slide carries `concentration.acquis: true`: the player reads the student's bilan (GET `/course-player/acquis/{deck}`), hands it to the simulation (`set_parameters {lacunes, acquis, acquis_ids}` — blocks ringed red « à reprendre » / orange « à revoir », green ✓ when acquired) and, when the voice ends, the tutor names the gaps aloud (live voice, `phraseDesLacunes`, from each competence's spoken name `competences[].dit`, no symbol) and freezes on `lacunes`. The written content (`bullets`) repeats every block; `cahier` lists all the formulas.
   - **« La carte du chapitre »** = a concept map (`ondes-tableau/carte.html`): the definition in the centre, 6–8 branches drawn one after the other at the rhythm of the voice (`play_until {moment: <branche>}`), each with its notion and its formula; the student touches a branch to read its sentence; the same `acquis` flag rings the branches to rework. Nothing scrolls: everything in the 760 × 420 frame (`__horsCadre` stays empty).
   - **« 🖨️ Imprimer / PDF »** on both (user rule, 8 November 2026): `shared/tableau/imprimer.js` renders every page of the simulation fully written (`scene.image()` from `commun.js`) in ink colours on white, shows a preview with « Imprimer / PDF » (the browser's print box, « Enregistrer au format PDF ») and one « Télécharger » PNG per page, then restores the scene; the player's iframe sandbox allows `allow-modals allow-downloads` for that. A new `public/` file is not seen by a running Vite dev server: restart it.
   - The fiche never rewrites itself when the voice moves on: each voiced block has its own writing timeline (`S.apparu[i]`), the unvoiced ones follow the natural order after the title.
   - Both slides belong to the « rédiger / bilan » competence and ask no question; the gaps shown are those the free mode will reuse (skill `suivi-des-acquis`).
10. **Check:** `python preparer_reponses.py verifier <cours.json>` must print « cours prêt pour le mode concentration » (every `mot` found, every track present, every question keyed, every reaction written and voiced). Then play the course in the browser (pane visible: a hidden pane slows the animations and falsifies the timings): freezes on their words, tutor in his column, questions at the end, reactions voiced.
11. **Show the user**, wait for validation; only then register the simulations as lesson resources, publish the deck, refresh the voice cache, deploy.

## Traps already met

- **Lip-sync**: the tutor's mouth follows the SOUND, through the shared analyser (`services/avatarAudio.ts`), not the « speaking » flag (which only moves the body). Every `<audio>` the tutor speaks through (course track, live voice, prepared reactions) must call `observeAvatarMedia` on play (8 November 2026: the course tracks never did, so he talked with his mouth shut). The analyser also retries once the AudioContext is running: a track that starts by itself before the context is up used to stay unconnected for its whole length. To test it, keep the browser pane visible: hidden, `requestAnimationFrame` stops and `data-mouth` stays at 0 whatever the voice does.

- A long all-French sentence makes the darija-trained voice mumble (« petit bruit, puis plus rien ») — cut it by commas and run `detecter_murmures.py`.
- A command cue leaves 0,9 s before its word (the tutor's flight to the button); a frozen scene must still draw what moves (`running || pause`).
- The in-app browser blocks sandboxed iframes without `allow-same-origin`; our own simulations are loaded with `allow-scripts allow-same-origin`.
- The local DeepSeek key is refused (401) and there is no logged-in student locally: test the LLM parts on the VPS, the screen with stubbed responses — or rely on the prepared reactions, which need no server.
