# Voix du cours

The voice is a Chatterbox model fine-tuned on Moroccan teachers, now **Bodi v6** (kernel `ferdaous04/academy-v6-bodi`, file `t3_mtl23ls_v2.safetensors`, `reference_bodi.flac`; chosen by the user by ear on 24 November 2026), checked by **MoulSot v5** (`zouhairessaihi/academy-moulsot-v5`, folder `moulsot_v5`). Both are the backend defaults (`kaggle_tts_modele`, `kaggle_stt_modele`, `kaggle_stt_source`) and are shared with the four Kaggle accounts (sharing is done by hand on kaggle.com, there is no API). It says well what it heard in training and badly what it never heard. The student must understand the darija AND learn the French he will write in tests and at the national exam. Rules validated by the user, 7 November 2026, on the waves example course.

## 1. Write the text

**Darija carries the explanation; French carries everything the student will write.**

- **French, always:**
  - the programme's technical terms (l'onde, la perturbation, la célérité, le retard, le front, l'élongation, l'aspect, la source, le milieu, transversale, longitudinale, la sensibilité, la division, l'oscilloscope…);
  - **numbers**, as BIOF teachers say them: deux, zéro virgule deux, trois cent quarante, mille cinq cents, deux fois dix puissance moins trois;
  - **never a darija number next to French, even inside a darija sentence** (user rule, 21 November 2026: « ne pas dire les chiffres en arabe + le mot en français, c'est mieux de dire le tout en français ; par exemple pour 2 noyaux il dit جوج noyaux »): « deux noyaux », not « جوج noyaux »; « trois courbes », not « تلاتة ديال les courbes »; « les deux courbes », not « بجوج courbes »; « deux fois », not « جوج مرات »; « zéro virgule zéro six mètre », not « زيرو فاصلة زيرو ستة mètre »; « six virgule cinq fois dix puissance moins sept », not « ستة فاصلة خمسة في عشرة أس moins سبعة ». « واحد » used as the article (« واحد la courbe », a curve) is not a number and stays. Check before generating: `python -X utf8 scripts/voix/nombres_fr.py <deck…>` lists what is left (`--ecrire` converts it in the course files); on 21 November 2026 it converted 1 165 sentences (687 tracks) in the 23 courses;
  - **never a darija word next to a French word inside a formula or a number either** (user rule, 22 November 2026, after hearing « 400–800 » badly said in physics ch3: « ne pas mélanger arabe et français dans deux mots successifs »). Operators are French: « x plus un », « k égale trois », « dix puissance moins cinq », « deux sur sept », « supérieur ou égal à moins un », « inférieur ou égal à », not « x زائد واحد », « k يساوي trois », « dix أس ناقص خمسة », « deux على sept », « كبر ولا يساوي moins un ». Hundreds written in one word are French too: « neuf cents nanomètres », not « تسعمية nanomètre ». Decimals said digit by digit stay French after « virgule »: « un virgule cinq sept », not « un virgule خمسة سبعة ». A count said wholly in darija (« جوج طرق ») is fine: the rule is only about two successive words, one darija and one French. **This is enforced in code**: `texte_parle.parle` runs `nombres_darija_fr` first, so every voice (course tracks, reactions, batches, cache of the board, the tutor's live replies) gets it even if a text slips through; still write it right in the course file, because the hash and the screen use the written text. On 22 November 2026, 833 tracks of the 23 courses were corrected this way and revoiced (v25);
  - **no darija word glued just before a French number** (user rule, 22 November 2026, physics ch3 `ol_r2b_celerite`: « تقريبا huit minutes » was said « thuit », « كيساويو un » was said « واحد »; « vérifier et élargir tous les audios avec ces problèmes, nous voulons améliorer la qualité de nos cours »): « environ huit minutes », not « تقريبا huit minutes »; « les termes égalent un », « deux égale un », not « كيساوي un » after a French word. `nombres_darija_fr` does it (`_nd_AVANT_NOMBRE`, `_nd_EGALE`); « شحال كتساوي g » stays (« شحال égale » would be another mix). The same liaison happens after « حتى », « بين … و », « من بعد »: « حتى huit » was said « تويت », « من بعد huit virgule trois » came out garbled — write the whole range or date in French: « de neuf à huit virgule un », « entre deux et huit virgule trois minutes », « après huit virgule trois minutes »;
  - **every French word fully in Latin letters, every darija word fully in Arabic letters.** Broken words such as « فالنoyau », « الدilution », « زéro », « مètre » (first letter in the wrong alphabet) or « Mزيان », « fين » are read as noise. `nombres_darija_fr` repairs the known ones; `python -X utf8 scripts/voix/nombres_fr.py <deck…>` lists what is left. Glued articles and darija-conjugated French verbs (« فla courbe », « الcourbe », « نétablir », « كيprédomine ») are the corpus style and stay;
  - **no classical-Arabic word where a darija word exists**: « نقل » (copy) was read badly in the notebook invitation n° 4 and became « كتب ». Before voicing, check rare words against the corpus (§ 2);
  - **units, always in French words, never the symbol and never in darija** (not « متر فالثانية », not « ميلي ثانية »): m/s → mètre par seconde, km/h → kilomètre par heure, s → seconde, ms → milliseconde, µs → microseconde, min → minute, h → heure, m → mètre, cm → centimètre, mm → millimètre, km → kilomètre, Hz → hertz, kHz → kilohertz, N → newton, J → joule, W → watt, V → volt, A → ampère, Ω → ohm, F → farad, °C → degré Celsius, K → kelvin, kg → kilogramme, g → gramme, mol → mole, mol/L → mole par litre, L → litre, Pa → pascal. A value is said with its unit: « trois cent quarante mètres par seconde », « quarante millisecondes », « zéro virgule cinq milliseconde par division »; the conversion too: « deux millisecondes égale deux fois dix puissance moins trois seconde »;
  - **no audible plural S.** In French the final s (or x) of a plural is silent, but the voice reads it when it has rarely heard the plural form: « les micros » came out « micross ». The text keeps correct French; the generator passes it through `corpus_v4/pluriels_muets.py`, which writes each French plural seen fewer than 50 times in the corpus in its singular spelling (« les micro », « les division »), so it is said as a teacher says it. Words whose final s is pronounced (plus, sens, bus, virus, hélas, os…) and plurals the corpus knows well (les, électrons, mètres…) are left alone;
  - **equations and operations**, said entirely in French: « v égale d sur delta t », « tau égale SM sur v », « L égale v fois teta », « deux d égale v fois tau, donc d égale v fois tau sur deux », « y de M de t égale y de S de t moins tau ». The words are plus, moins, fois, sur (or divisé par), égale, puissance, au carré, racine carrée, delta;
  - **« en fonction de »** for every graph or relation: « y en fonction de t », « y en fonction de x », « delta t en fonction de d »;
  - **the exam words**, so the student hears them before reading them in a test: calculer, déterminer, déduire, justifier, montrer que, représenter, l'expression, la relation, l'application numérique, la valeur, le résultat, vrai, faux;
  - the simple French words every student already uses: donc, alors, voilà, d'accord, c'est, bien, déjà.
- **Simple darija for everything else.** A French word that is none of the above (« la bouée », « la queue », « la manip », « clap », « impulsion », « le barème », « l'énoncé », « le trajet ») was never heard by the voice and many students do not know it: say it in everyday darija (« واحد الكورة صغيرة فوق الما », « اللخر ديال la perturbation », « فالتجربة », « كنضرب بيدي », « السؤال »).
- **Common darija, short sentences.** One idea per sentence, verb near the start. Prefer the words teachers use most in the corpus: يعني، دابا، إذن، هادي، شنو، كيفاش، علاش، واش، باش، شحال، مثلا، مزيان، بحال، ملي، **يلا** (not « إلا »). Avoid rare or literary words and long glued forms (« فالمراية », « مزحزحين », « كتحسم », « وكيبعدو » never occur in the corpus).
- **No tashkil, ever.** The corpus has none; harakat push the model off its training and make the word worse. A word that seems to need vowels is replaced by a simpler one (or said in French if it is a programme term).
- **Symbols as the corpus writes them:** teta (not thêta), tau, delta t, lambda, y. Letters for points as they are: S, M, A.
- **Definitions to learn by heart** are re-read in French, word for word, after « نعاود نقرا : » (professeur skill, rule 5). **Cut every French sentence longer than about ten words with commas** (« le point M reproduit le mouvement de la source, avec un retard tau, égal à SM sur v »): the generator makes one piece per comma group, and a long all-French piece is where the darija-trained voice loses its way.
- **No symbols, no digits, no markdown** in the text: everything is written as it is said (`texte_parle.py` turns what is left into words, but the text should not rely on it).

## 2. Check every word against the training corpus

```bash
python C:/Users/HP/Desktop/voxtts/corpus_v4/verifier_texte_corpus.py <cours.json>
```

It reads every `speech_text.mixed`, applies `texte_parle.py`, and lists French words seen fewer than 20 times that are not programme or exam words, and darija words seen fewer than 3 times. Replace them with corpus words (look a candidate up the same way before using it) until the list is empty or only holds words you keep on purpose (a programme term the student must hear for the bac, said with its article and explained once in darija). The corpus file is `corpus_v4/selection_train.jsonl` (dataset `zouhairessaihi/academy-v4-selection`, 38 852 sentences, 28 % French words).

Measured on the waves example: rewriting with these rules raised the words recognised by transcription from 67 % to 78 % on average, and the worst slide from 50 % to 69 %.

## 3 bis. The number check is compulsory, in batches AND in live replies (user rule, 24 November 2026)

« c'est bien marché, veuillez ajouter tous ces éléments en skill de génération d'audios pour tous les prochains cours et aussi dans les réponses libres exiger de le faire ».

- **Where the voice fails**: a French number placed right after an Arabic word, above all after « و », « ل », « من », « حتى ل », « يعني ». Measured on 24/11/2026: « يعني vingt-deux nucléons، و vingt-deux » → the second one heard « و ألف »; « حتى ل huit cents » → « douze cents »; « من quatre cents » garbled; « السؤال un » / « السؤال cinq » said in darija. A sentence that starts with the number (« neuf cents nanomètres كبر من… ») is said right. Write numbers so they do not follow an Arabic word when you can (§ 1), but **punctuation is not a fix**: a comma, a full stop or generating the number as a separate piece were tested on 5 sentences and none worked every time (the full stop fixed one and broke two). The fix is the check below, with **takes that change the way of saying it**, plus listening.
- **Retake strategies** (user's choice by ear, 24 November 2026, on 5 sentences × 4 versions): take 1 is the normal text; if a number is wrong, take 2 is **D** — the piece is cut just before the FIRST French number that follows an Arabic word, both parts are generated separately and joined with 0,12 s of breath (« le rouge كيوقف عند | huit cents nanomètres… », « فيه | quatorze protons… »); if it is still wrong, take 3 is **C** — a full stop between that Arabic word and the number (« يعني. vingt-deux nucléons… »). The user heard D right where C failed (« huit cents », « quatorze ») and C right where D failed (« يعني vingt-deux »); neither breaks a sentence that was already right. Only the first number of the piece is changed, as in the versions the user validated. Code: `coupe_d`, `variante_c`, `prise` in the `LOT` block of `lot_sans_tunnel.py`; `rapport.json` gives the kept way in `strategie` (normal, D, C).
- **The check** (`fautes_nombres(stt, texte)`, in `backend/kaggle_tts/serveur.py` and in the `LOT` block of `/srv/corpus_v4/voix_lot/lot_sans_tunnel.py`): MoulSot's transcript and the written text are both turned into their sequence of French number words — digits and decimals written by MoulSot (« 22 », « 6,02 », « 37% ») become words with `num2words`, darija numbers (« جوج », « خمسة », « ألف »…) count as their French equivalent — and compared with `difflib`. A missing, extra or changed number is `NOMBRES a / b`; a number heard in darija that the text writes in French is `DARIJA …`. It is added to `defauts()`, so **a batch take with a number fault is redone (3 takes at most: normal, D, C)** and the best take is kept; `rapport.json` keeps what is left in `reste`. On the 22 test sentences: 8 faulty takes kept before, 1 after.
- **Live replies (free questions)**: the live kernel checks every piece that holds a French number with the same function and **regenerates it up to 2 times** before sending it (log « nombres, morceau refait »); pieces without a number skip the transcription, so they are not delayed. Batches pass `controle_nombres: False` because they check themselves. Test: `test_controle_des_nombres_de_la_voix_en_direct` in `app/tests/test_kaggle_tts.py`. A change reaches the live voice when `academy-tts-direct` is pushed again.
- **Whenever you compare transcripts** (benches, reports), convert digits, decimal commas and « % » to words first: MoulSot writes « 22 nucléons », and a raw CER counts it as wrong (0,43 on a correct sentence). MoulSot is both the judge and, for MoulSot models, the candidate: its scores point where to listen, **the user decides by ear**.
- **Listening pages**: the Claude app preview does not play audio embedded in a page. Build a self-contained HTML (each track as MP3 64 kb/s in base64, text, transcript, takes, faults left) and publish it on the VPS under an unguessable path `/var/www/moalim/_ecoute_<random>/`, then give the link to open in Chrome.

## 4. Check what the voice actually says

- Audio sanity: no clipping, longest silence under 2 s, and **no mumble**: `python C:/Users/HP/Desktop/voxtts/corpus_v4/detecter_murmures.py <dossier des wav>` must print only `ok`. A mumble is the voice dropping out mid-sentence: one second or more of low, dull sound (between -52 and -30 dB, neither speech nor silence) before the voice comes back. The student hears a small noise, then nothing, then the voice again. Met on 8 November 2026 in `wx_r1_retard_v4.wav` (40,2 → 43,6 s): the long all-French re-read « … avec un retard tau égal à SM sur v » came out as « T ممم تا ممها ». The transcription shows it too (« ممم », words missing at the end of a French sentence). Regenerate that slide with another seed after cutting the sentence with commas; never place a track that still has a mumble.
- Transcription with MoulSot (`atlasia/moulsot.v0.3`, darija ASR) on Kaggle: `corpus_v4/kaggle_verif_moulsot.py` (kernel source = the generation kernel), cut at silences into ≤ 20 s pieces. Compare the Arabic words of the spoken text with the transcription, slide by slide; list the passages of 3 or more words that are missing. MoulSot hears French badly and writes numbers as digits, so a low score is a pointer for listening, not a verdict.
- **The numbers heard must be the numbers written, for EVERY track** (user rule, 22 November 2026: « ici dans le titre il lit l'iode 131, il dit cent trente wahad en darija… il doit lire tout en français » ; « pas seulement wahd, chercher les autres chiffres en arabe »). Even with a perfect text, Bodi sometimes says a French number in darija: « cent trente et un » → « … واحد », « cinq » → « خمسة », « sept » → « سبعة », « cent pour cent » → « مية فالمية », « huit » → « thuit ». On 22/11/2026, 34 of 2 204 transcribed tracks had it. So every new or regenerated track is transcribed (the batch kernel `lot_sans_tunnel.py` keeps MoulSot's text in `rapport.json`; for tracks already published, `/srv/corpus_v4/voix_lot/stt_seul.py` + `lancer_stt.sh N pousser LISTE` transcribe them from moalim.online without regenerating), then text and transcription are **aligned word by word** (`difflib` on `parle(texte)` vs STT tokens): a French number word replaced by a darija number (واحد, جوج, تلاتة … خمسة, سبعة, مية, ألف, فاصلة…) is a fault — regenerate the track (new take) and check again; keep the take whose numbers match. Also compare the sequence of French number words (text vs STT): a different number (« six cent cinquante » heard « cinq cent cinquante ») is a fault too.
- Give the user a **listening page** (track, text, transcription, score, « Bien / À refaire ») and redo only the slides they mark.

## 5. Same rules for the tutor's live replies (free questions)

The live voice (`backend/kaggle_tts/serveur.py`, kernel `academy-tts-direct`) uses the same model, so it fails the same way:
- **numbers and formulas**: every piece with a French number is transcribed and regenerated up to 2 times if a number is wrong or said in darija (§ 3 bis); the kernel's `parle` applies `nombres_darija_fr` to every live reply, and the prompt (`llm_service.py`) asks the model to write « x plus un », « deux noyaux », never « x زائد واحد », « جوج noyaux » (§ 1). A change to these rules reaches the live voice only when the kernel is pushed again (`construire_kernel` embeds `texte_parle.py`);
- the prompt ([RÈGLE DE SÉCURITÉ — PRONONCIATION ET FRANÇAIS] in `llm_service.py`) tells the model to cut every all-French sentence longer than ten words with commas;
- each piece is checked with `murmure()` as soon as it is generated; a piece that mumbles is generated once more before it is sent (log line « murmure, morceau refait »). The test is `test_un_morceau_qui_murmure_est_refait` in `app/tests/test_kaggle_tts.py`;
- the course generator (`gen_kaggle_cours_exemple.py`) does the same with up to 3 new seeds, and `seules=<id,id>` regenerates only the listed slides.
- the tutor's PREPARED reactions to every possible answer (professeur skill, rule 20) are voiced by the same generator: `python gen_kaggle_cours_exemple.py reponses=<pistes.json>` (a list of `{id, parole}`, kernel `ferdaous04/academy-cours-reponses`); the `parole` texts follow the rules of section 1 (they are written by DeepSeek with these rules in its prompt, then checked: no digits, no symbols). Tracks go to `/media/audio/courses/<deck>/reponses/<id>_v<N>.wav` and their url into the reaction (`audio`).

## 6. Place the tracks in the course

`corpus_v4/poser_pistes_exemple.py <output dir> <version>` copies each `<slide id>.wav` only if its text hash matches the current `speech_text.mixed`, and writes `slide.audio.mixed = {url, duration_ms, version, speech_hash, status}`. Bump the version in the file name (`_v3.wav`, …) at each regeneration so browsers do not replay a cached old track, and delete the previous files.
